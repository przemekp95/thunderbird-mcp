Fixed safety policy

  • Mail: bounded reads, HTML/raw access, saved attachments, drafts, reviewed compose/reply/forward, separately authorized direct sending, organization, and Trash-only deletion.
  • Contacts: search, read, create, and update inside explicitly selected address books; permanent contact deletion is unavailable.
  • Email and contact fields are untrusted data with control-character cleanup and size limits.
  • Direct sending is hidden unless locally enabled and remains limited to selected accounts and recipients, three inline attachments, per-message recipient caps, and a daily attempt quota.
  • Calendars, filters, permanent deletion, and host-path attachments are unavailable.
  • The bearer token rotates whenever the extension server starts.
  • Automatic add-on updates are disabled for this local fork.
